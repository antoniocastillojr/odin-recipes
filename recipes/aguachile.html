Aguachile

Description

Aguachile is a fresh, spicy Mexican seafood dish made with raw shrimp submerged in a bright, fiery blend of lime juice and pulverized green chilis.

Ingredients

  • Raw shrimp
  • Serrano chili peppers
  • Lime juice
  • Red onion
  • Avocados
  • Cucumber
  • Salt
  • Tostadas

Steps

  1. Prepare the shrimp by placing them in lime juice and salt until no longer raw. They will be a bright pink color.
  2. Blend lime juice, serrano chili peppers, and cilantro for the marinade.
  3. Marinade the prepared shrimp and refrigerate for 30 minutes so flavors mix.
  4. Serve with tostadas.
