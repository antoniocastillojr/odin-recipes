Birria

Description

Birria is a rich, Mexican meat stew made with beef that is marinated in dried chiles and spices, then slow-cooked until very tender.

Ingredients

  • Dried chilis: guajo, ancho, and arbol
  • Spices: garlic, cumin, clove, peppercorns, oregano, cinnamon, and bay leaves
  • Beef chunk roast
  • Beef broth
  • Vinegar
  • Tomato, garlic, and onion

Steps

  1. Season and sear meat over medium-high heat with olive oil.
  2. Boil & cook the dried chilis, tomatoes, onion, cinnamon, peppercorn, and bay leaves for 10 minutes.
  3. Blend the cooked chilis, spices, and vegetables with the broth, vinegar, and remaining spices.
  4. Strain the blended sauce into the pot with your seared meat, then boil for 3-3.5 hours until tenderized.
  5. Remove the meat, shred it, then add it back to the sauce/stew.
  6. Serve with tortillas.
